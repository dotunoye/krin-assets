Made for real family life

Reliable care. Meaningful afternoons.

The Haven bridges the gap between the school bell and home time with intentional rhythms that meet children where they are. We turn stressful homework hours into structured progress.

  • Calm transition and nourishing snack
  • Guided homework and reading support
  • Creative labs, movement, and outdoor play
  • Small-group character and faith moments
  • Flexible pickup windows for working families
A typical day

From school shoes to confident smiles.

2:00 PM

Welcome & exhale

Check-in, refreshments, and a calm space to rest and quietly reset after the school day.

3:30 PM

Rest & Reset

A designated period to relax, read, reflect, and socialize appropriately before focus time.

4:30 PM

Enrichment & Exploration

Guided homework assistance, followed by rotating STEM, arts, music, and digital literacy activities.

5:30 PM

Structured Free Play

Indoor or outdoor movement, team-building challenges, and cooperative games under supervision.

6:00 PM

Parent Pick-Up

Verified handover. Instead of rushing through assignments at home, you get to enjoy your evening.

Holistic Growth

Our Program Pillars.

We blend structured academic support with creative exploration, ensuring children thrive intellectually and emotionally.

Academic Support

Guided homework sessions, foundational reading reinforcement, and the gentle development of independent study habits.

STEM & Tech Literacy

Hands-on robotics experiments, practical typing skills, internet safety, and interactive problem-solving challenges.

Creative Arts & Teamwork

Expressive freedom through music, crafts, and group activities that nurture communication, conflict resolution, and confidence.

Structured Curriculum

A new discovery every day.

Our weekly rhythm balances deep focus with creative discovery, ensuring every afternoon offers a unique opportunity to grow.

  • Monday: Literacy & Reading Lab (Storytelling & Vocab)
  • Tuesday: Tech & Computer Literacy (Typing & Safety)
  • Wednesday: STEM Discovery (Science & Math Games)
  • Thursday: Creative Arts & Music (Rhythm & Crafts)
  • Friday: Life Skills & Leadership (Team Challenges)
